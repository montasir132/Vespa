🚀 Vespa - Business Landing Page
Vespa is a modern, responsive, and visually appealing landing page designed for digital agencies, design studios, and technology startups to showcase their services, portfolio, and pricing plans.

🛠️ Technology Stack

HTML5 - Semantic structure and layout

CSS3 - Custom styling and design responsiveness

Bootstrap 5 - Responsive grid system, layout, and components

JavaScript (ES6) - Interactive components and smooth scroll effects

✨ Key Features

📱 Fully Responsive: Seamlessly adapts to mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

💼 Services & Features Section: Showcase what your business offers clearly.

📊 Stat Counters: Display total projects, happy clients, awards, and years of experience.

💬 Testimonial Slider: Client reviews and feedback layout.

📁 Portfolio Showcase: Grid section to display recent apps and work samples.

👥 Team Profiles: Introduce core team members and their roles.

🏷️ Pricing Tables: Clear pricing plans (Free, Business, Developer).

❓ FAQ Accordion: Frequently asked questions section.

✉️ Contact Form: Clean contact form layout with location details.

📦 Dependencies

Bootstrap

Font Awesome / Bootstrap Icons (For icon assets)
